# Code of conduct

Kirikae is a technical project, and its spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) exist to build it.

- Be respectful and patient. Critique code and ideas, never people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Stay on topic. Do not ask for, offer or link games, keys, firmware or piracy sites (see [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md)).
- Assume good faith; many contributors are writing in their second language.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and block people who break these rules. To report a
problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
